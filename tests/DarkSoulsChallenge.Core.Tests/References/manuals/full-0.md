# Dark Souls challenge — seed full-0

- **Class:** Bandit
- **Starting gift:** Divine Blessing x3
- **Soul level cap:** 63
- **Difficulty:** easy · **Curve:** rollercoaster · **Length:** full

Follow every order. Break one, and the challenge is over: there is no penalty, only the end.

## Rules of the journey

1. **Phase swap.** After the Asylum Demon and until you have rung both bells: only swords. After it: drop what does not fit and follow only thrusting swords.
2. **Order of the Lords.** Collect the four Lord Souls in this order: Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos), then Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath), then Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings), then Lord Soul (Nito).
3. **Earned upgrades.** Your weapon may gain only one upgrade level per boss you defeat.

## Chapter 1: The Asylum

Until you defeat Asylum Demon · **Areas:** Northern Undead Asylum · **Bosses:** Asylum Demon · **Difficulty:** easy

4. **One bonfire.** In Northern Undead Asylum: rest only at the Undead Asylum - Courtyard Bonfire.

## Chapter 2: The first bell

Until you ring the bell of the Undead Parish · **Areas:** Darkroot Basin, Darkroot Garden, Depths, Firelink Shrine, Undead Burg, Undead Parish · **Bosses:** Bell Gargoyles, Capra Demon, Gaping Dragon, Moonlight Butterfly, Stray Demon, Taurus Demon · **Difficulty:** medium

5. **Boss before the bell.** Before you ring the first bell, defeat Stray Demon.
6. **Every chest.** In Firelink Shrine: open all 4 chests before you ring the bell of the Undead Parish.

## Chapter 3: The second bell

Until you have rung both bells · **Areas:** Blighttown, Quelaag's Domain, The Great Hollow · **Bosses:** Chaos Witch Quelaag · **Difficulty:** easy

7. **Nothing from the ground.** In Blighttown: nothing you pick up from a corpse or chest may be used or equipped. _Drop anything that is not allowed as soon as you pick it up, so you do not mix it up later._
8. **Errand.** Before you have rung both bells: take the Wanderer Boots found in Blighttown.

## Chapter 4: The Lordvessel

Until you place the Lordvessel · **Areas:** Anor Londo, Firelink Altar, Sanctuary Garden, Sen's Fortress · **Bosses:** Gwyndolin, Iron Golem, Ornstein and Smough, Sanctuary Guardian · **Difficulty:** medium

9. **Wear what you find.** In Sen's Fortress: every armor piece you pick up must be put on at once, and stays on until you leave.
10. **Chapter restriction.** From the moment you have rung both bells until you place the Lordvessel: do not use consumable items.

## Chapter 5: Lord Soul: Bed of Chaos

Until you place the Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos) · **Areas:** Demon Ruins, Lost Izalith, Valley of Drakes · **Bosses:** Bed of Chaos, Ceaseless Discharge, Centipede Demon, Demon Firesage · **Difficulty:** easy

11. **One bonfire.** In Lost Izalith: rest only at the Lost Izalith - Past Illusory Wall Bonfire.

## Chapter 6: Lord Soul: Seath

Until you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath) · **Areas:** Ash Lake, Crystal Cave, The Duke's Archives · **Bosses:** Seath the Scaleless · **Difficulty:** medium

12. **No bonfire.** Do not rest at any bonfire in Ash Lake.
13. **Errand.** Before you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath): take the Miracle: Great Magic Barrier found in Ash Lake.

## Chapter 7: Lord Soul: Four Kings

Until you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings) · **Areas:** New Londo Ruins, The Abyss · **Bosses:** Four Kings, Great Grey Wolf Sif · **Difficulty:** easy

14. **Nothing from the ground.** In New Londo Ruins: nothing you pick up from a corpse or chest may be used or equipped. _Drop anything that is not allowed as soon as you pick it up, so you do not mix it up later._
15. **Counted attempts.** You have 11 attempts against Great Grey Wolf Sif.

## Chapter 8: Lord Soul: Nito

Until you place the Lord Soul (Nito) · **Areas:** Chasm of the Abyss, Oolacile Sanctuary, Oolacile Township, Painted World of Ariamis, Royal Wood, The Catacombs, Tomb of the Giants · **Bosses:** Black Dragon Kalameet, Crossbreed Priscilla, Gravelord Nito, Artorias, Manus, Father of the Abyss, Pinwheel · **Difficulty:** medium

16. **Dry fight.** Against Artorias: do not use Estus.
17. **Chapter restriction.** From the moment you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings) until you place the Lord Soul (Nito): do not lock on to enemies.

## Chapter 9: The First Flame

Until you defeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Areas:** Kiln of the First Flame · **Bosses:** Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Difficulty:** easy

18. **Wear what you find.** In Kiln of the First Flame: every armor piece you pick up must be put on at once, and stays on until you leave.

## Definitions

- **Attempt:** each fight against a boss that you start, whether you win, die or leave.
- **Pick up:** take an item from a corpse or a chest in the world. What enemies drop, what you buy and what characters give you are not picked up.
- **Use:** equip, wear, wield, cast or consume.
- **Chest:** a treasure chest placed in the world. A mimic is not one, even though it looks like one.

## Before you go

- **Darkstalker Kaathe:** He appears in the Abyss after the Four Kings only if the Lordvessel is not placed yet. Refuse him or attack him, and he leaves for good.
- **Lautrec of Carim:** Once both bells are rung, or both Fire Keeper Souls (Undead Parish and Blighttown) are taken, Anastacia is killed and Lautrec leaves Firelink, whether or not you freed him. The Firelink Shrine bonfire stays out until her soul is returned to her, a quest that starts with the Black Eye Orb.
- **Trusty Patches:** In the Tomb of the Giants, once Pinwheel is dead or Rhea has set out there, he waits by a ledge and kicks you into the pit below. It is a cutscene, not a death.
- **Rhea of Thorolund:** Once rescued in the Tomb of the Giants, she sells in the Undead Parish. She is lost if two more main bosses die before you visit her, and she leaves once her whole stock is bought.
- **Solaire of Astora:** In Lost Izalith, kill the four Chaos Bugs before you speak to him there, or he is lost to the Sunlight Maggot.
