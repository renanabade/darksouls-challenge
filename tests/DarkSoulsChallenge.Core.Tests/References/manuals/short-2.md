# Dark Souls challenge — seed short-2

- **Class:** Knight
- **Starting gift:** Divine Blessing x3
- **Soul level cap:** 22
- **Difficulty:** hard · **Curve:** rising · **Length:** short

Follow every order. Break one, and the challenge is over: there is no penalty, only the end.

## Rules of the journey

1. **Lives for the run.** You have 12 deaths for the whole run. Scripted deaths you cannot avoid do not count.
2. **Phase swap.** After the Asylum Demon and until you ring the bell of the Undead Parish: only swords. After it: drop what does not fit and follow only spears.
3. **Order of the Lords.** Collect the four Lord Souls in this order: Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings), then Lord Soul (Nito), then Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos), then Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath).

## Act 1: The first bell

Until you ring the bell of the Undead Parish · **Areas:** Darkroot Garden, Depths, Firelink Shrine, Northern Undead Asylum, Undead Burg, Undead Parish · **Bosses:** Asylum Demon, Bell Gargoyles, Capra Demon, Gaping Dragon, Moonlight Butterfly, Stray Demon, Taurus Demon · **Difficulty:** medium

4. **One bonfire.** In Undead Burg: rest only at the Sunlight Altar Bonfire.
5. **Errand.** Before you ring the bell of the Undead Parish: take the Knight Shield found in Undead Parish.

## Act 2: The Lordvessel

Until you place the Lordvessel · **Areas:** Anor Londo, Blighttown, Firelink Altar, Quelaag's Domain, Sen's Fortress, Valley of Drakes · **Bosses:** Chaos Witch Quelaag, Gwyndolin, Iron Golem, Ornstein and Smough · **Difficulty:** hard

6. **Dry fight.** Against Ornstein and Smough: do not use Estus.

## Act 3: The Lord Souls

Until you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath) · **Areas:** Ash Lake, Chasm of the Abyss, Crystal Cave, Darkroot Basin, Demon Ruins, Lost Izalith, New Londo Ruins, Oolacile Sanctuary, Oolacile Township, Painted World of Ariamis, Royal Wood, Sanctuary Garden, The Abyss, The Catacombs, The Duke's Archives, The Great Hollow, Tomb of the Giants · **Bosses:** Bed of Chaos, Black Dragon Kalameet, Ceaseless Discharge, Centipede Demon, Crossbreed Priscilla, Demon Firesage, Four Kings, Gravelord Nito, Great Grey Wolf Sif, Artorias, Manus, Father of the Abyss, Pinwheel, Sanctuary Guardian, Seath the Scaleless · **Difficulty:** hard

7. **No bonfire.** Do not rest at any bonfire in The Abyss.
8. **Counted attempts.** You have 5 attempts against Centipede Demon.

## Act 4: The First Flame

Until you defeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Areas:** Kiln of the First Flame · **Bosses:** Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Difficulty:** extreme

9. **Counted attempts.** You have 1 attempt against Gwyn, Lord of Cinder.
10. **Chapter restriction.** From the moment you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath) until you defeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder: do not cast sorceries, miracles or pyromancies.

## Definitions

- **Death:** every time you die. The only one that does not count is your first death against Seath the Scaleless in The Duke's Archives, which the story requires.
- **Attempt:** each fight against a boss that you start, whether you win, die or leave.

## Before you go

- **Darkstalker Kaathe:** He appears in the Abyss after the Four Kings only if the Lordvessel is not placed yet. Refuse him or attack him, and he leaves for good.
- **Lautrec of Carim:** Once both bells are rung, or both Fire Keeper Souls (Undead Parish and Blighttown) are taken, Anastacia is killed and Lautrec leaves Firelink, whether or not you freed him. The Firelink Shrine bonfire stays out until her soul is returned to her, a quest that starts with the Black Eye Orb.
- **Trusty Patches:** In the Tomb of the Giants, once Pinwheel is dead or Rhea has set out there, he waits by a ledge and kicks you into the pit below. It is a cutscene, not a death.
- **Rhea of Thorolund:** Once rescued in the Tomb of the Giants, she sells in the Undead Parish. She is lost if two more main bosses die before you visit her, and she leaves once her whole stock is bought.
- **Solaire of Astora:** In Lost Izalith, kill the four Chaos Bugs before you speak to him there, or he is lost to the Sunlight Maggot.
