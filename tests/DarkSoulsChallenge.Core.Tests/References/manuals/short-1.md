# Dark Souls challenge — seed short-1

- **Class:** Deprived
- **Starting gift:** Black Firebomb x10
- **Soul level cap:** 51
- **Difficulty:** medium · **Curve:** peaks · **Length:** short

Follow every order. Break one, and the challenge is over: there is no penalty, only the end.

## Rules of the journey

1. **Lives for the run.** You have 28 deaths for the whole run. Scripted deaths you cannot avoid do not count.
2. **Order of the Lords.** Collect the four Lord Souls in this order: Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings), then Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath), then Lord Soul (Nito), then Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos).
3. **Earned levels.** You may only level up with points. Each boss you defeat without drinking Estus is worth 3 points; one point, one level.
4. **Earned upgrades.** Your weapon may gain only one upgrade level per boss you defeat.

## Act 1: The first bell

Until you ring the bell of the Undead Parish · **Areas:** Darkroot Basin, Darkroot Garden, Firelink Shrine, Northern Undead Asylum, Undead Burg, Undead Parish · **Bosses:** Asylum Demon, Bell Gargoyles, Capra Demon, Moonlight Butterfly, Stray Demon, Taurus Demon · **Difficulty:** medium

5. **Dry fight.** Against Stray Demon: do not use Estus.

## Act 2: The Lordvessel

Until you place the Lordvessel · **Areas:** Anor Londo, Blighttown, Depths, Firelink Altar, Oolacile Sanctuary, Quelaag's Domain, Sanctuary Garden, Sen's Fortress · **Bosses:** Chaos Witch Quelaag, Gaping Dragon, Gwyndolin, Iron Golem, Ornstein and Smough, Sanctuary Guardian · **Difficulty:** extreme

6. **Counted attempts.** You have 1 attempt against Iron Golem.

## Act 3: The Lord Souls

Until you place the Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos) · **Areas:** Ash Lake, Chasm of the Abyss, Crystal Cave, Demon Ruins, Lost Izalith, New Londo Ruins, Oolacile Township, Painted World of Ariamis, Royal Wood, The Abyss, The Catacombs, The Duke's Archives, The Great Hollow, Tomb of the Giants · **Bosses:** Bed of Chaos, Black Dragon Kalameet, Ceaseless Discharge, Centipede Demon, Crossbreed Priscilla, Demon Firesage, Four Kings, Gravelord Nito, Great Grey Wolf Sif, Artorias, Manus, Father of the Abyss, Pinwheel, Seath the Scaleless · **Difficulty:** medium

7. **Lizard hunt.** In The Great Hollow: kill every crystal lizard before you place the Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos).
8. **The other way.** Until you place the Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos): do not enter Valley of Drakes.

## Act 4: The First Flame

Until you defeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Areas:** Kiln of the First Flame · **Bosses:** Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Difficulty:** medium

9. **Counted attempts.** You have 7 attempts against Gwyn, Lord of Cinder.

## Definitions

- **Death:** every time you die. The only one that does not count is your first death against Seath the Scaleless in The Duke's Archives, which the story requires.
- **Attempt:** each fight against a boss that you start, whether you win, die or leave.

## Before you go

- **Darkstalker Kaathe:** He appears in the Abyss after the Four Kings only if the Lordvessel is not placed yet. Refuse him or attack him, and he leaves for good.
- **Lautrec of Carim:** Once both bells are rung, or both Fire Keeper Souls (Undead Parish and Blighttown) are taken, Anastacia is killed and Lautrec leaves Firelink, whether or not you freed him. The Firelink Shrine bonfire stays out until her soul is returned to her, a quest that starts with the Black Eye Orb.
- **Trusty Patches:** In the Tomb of the Giants, once Pinwheel is dead or Rhea has set out there, he waits by a ledge and kicks you into the pit below. It is a cutscene, not a death.
- **Rhea of Thorolund:** Once rescued in the Tomb of the Giants, she sells in the Undead Parish. She is lost if two more main bosses die before you visit her, and she leaves once her whole stock is bought.
- **Solaire of Astora:** In Lost Izalith, kill the four Chaos Bugs before you speak to him there, or he is lost to the Sunlight Maggot.
