# Dark Souls challenge — seed short-3

- **Class:** Warrior
- **Starting gift:** Master Key
- **Soul level cap:** 9
- **Difficulty:** extreme · **Curve:** peaks · **Length:** short

Follow every order. Break one, and the challenge is over: there is no penalty, only the end.

## Rules of the journey

1. **Accumulated curse.** Each boss you defeat adds a new restriction, in this order: (1) Do not wear rings; (2) Do not buff your weapon or yourself; (3) Never hold a weapon with both hands; (4) Do not roll; (5) Do not reinforce your weapons. Allowed, because the run needs them: Covenant of Artorias, Orange Charred Ring.
2. **Order of the Lords.** Collect the four Lord Souls in this order: Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath), then Lord Soul (Nito), then Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos), then Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings).
3. **Earned levels.** You may only level up with points. Each boss you defeat without summoning help is worth 1 point; one point, one level.
4. **Earned upgrades.** Your weapon may gain only one upgrade level per boss you defeat.

## Act 1: The first bell

Until you ring the bell of the Undead Parish · **Areas:** Darkroot Garden, Firelink Shrine, Northern Undead Asylum, Undead Burg, Undead Parish · **Bosses:** Asylum Demon, Bell Gargoyles, Capra Demon, Moonlight Butterfly, Stray Demon, Taurus Demon · **Difficulty:** extreme

5. **Boss before the bell.** Before you ring the first bell, defeat Capra Demon.

## Act 2: The Lordvessel

Until you place the Lordvessel · **Areas:** Anor Londo, Blighttown, Depths, Firelink Altar, Quelaag's Domain, Sen's Fortress, Valley of Drakes · **Bosses:** Chaos Witch Quelaag, Gaping Dragon, Gwyndolin, Iron Golem, Ornstein and Smough · **Difficulty:** hard

6. **Dry fight.** Against Gwyndolin: do not use Estus.

## Act 3: The Lord Souls

Until you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings) · **Areas:** Ash Lake, Chasm of the Abyss, Crystal Cave, Darkroot Basin, Demon Ruins, Lost Izalith, New Londo Ruins, Oolacile Sanctuary, Oolacile Township, Painted World of Ariamis, Royal Wood, Sanctuary Garden, The Abyss, The Catacombs, The Duke's Archives, The Great Hollow, Tomb of the Giants · **Bosses:** Bed of Chaos, Black Dragon Kalameet, Ceaseless Discharge, Centipede Demon, Crossbreed Priscilla, Demon Firesage, Four Kings, Gravelord Nito, Great Grey Wolf Sif, Artorias, Manus, Father of the Abyss, Pinwheel, Sanctuary Guardian, Seath the Scaleless · **Difficulty:** extreme

7. **Chapter restriction.** From the moment you place the Lordvessel until you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings): do not wear armor.

## Act 4: The First Flame

Until you defeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Areas:** Kiln of the First Flame · **Bosses:** Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Difficulty:** extreme

8. **Chapter restriction.** From the moment you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings) until you defeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder: do not drink Estus.

## Definitions

- **Use:** equip, wear, wield, cast or consume.

## Before you go

- **Darkstalker Kaathe:** He appears in the Abyss after the Four Kings only if the Lordvessel is not placed yet. Refuse him or attack him, and he leaves for good.
- **Lautrec of Carim:** Once both bells are rung, or both Fire Keeper Souls (Undead Parish and Blighttown) are taken, Anastacia is killed and Lautrec leaves Firelink, whether or not you freed him. The Firelink Shrine bonfire stays out until her soul is returned to her, a quest that starts with the Black Eye Orb.
- **Trusty Patches:** In the Tomb of the Giants, once Pinwheel is dead or Rhea has set out there, he waits by a ledge and kicks you into the pit below. It is a cutscene, not a death.
- **Rhea of Thorolund:** Once rescued in the Tomb of the Giants, she sells in the Undead Parish. She is lost if two more main bosses die before you visit her, and she leaves once her whole stock is bought.
- **Solaire of Astora:** In Lost Izalith, kill the four Chaos Bugs before you speak to him there, or he is lost to the Sunlight Maggot.
