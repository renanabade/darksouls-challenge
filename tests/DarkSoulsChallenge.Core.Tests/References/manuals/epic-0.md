# Dark Souls challenge — seed epic-0

- **Class:** Sorcerer
- **Starting gift:** Pendant
- **Soul level cap:** 70
- **Difficulty:** easy · **Curve:** rising · **Length:** epic

Follow every order. Break one, and the challenge is over: there is no penalty, only the end.

## Rules of the journey

1. **Order of the Lords.** Collect the four Lord Souls in this order: Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath), then Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos), then Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings), then Lord Soul (Nito).
2. **Forbidden area.** Do not enter Darkroot Basin.
3. **Earned levels.** You may only level up with points. Each boss you defeat without drinking Estus is worth 3 points; one point, one level.
4. **Earned upgrades.** Your weapon may gain only one upgrade level per boss you defeat.

## Chapter 1: The Asylum

Until you defeat Asylum Demon · **Areas:** Northern Undead Asylum · **Bosses:** Asylum Demon · **Difficulty:** easy

5. **Errand.** Before you defeat Asylum Demon: take the Rusted Iron Ring found in Northern Undead Asylum.

## Chapter 2: The first bell

Until you ring the bell of the Undead Parish · **Areas:** Darkroot Garden, Firelink Shrine, Undead Burg, Undead Parish · **Bosses:** Bell Gargoyles, Capra Demon, Moonlight Butterfly, Stray Demon, Taurus Demon · **Difficulty:** easy

6. **Wear what you find.** In Undead Burg: every armor piece you pick up must be put on at once, and stays on until you leave.
7. **No bonfire.** Do not rest at any bonfire in Undead Parish.
8. **One bonfire.** In Undead Burg: rest only at the Sunlight Altar Bonfire.

## Chapter 3: The second bell

Until you have rung both bells · **Areas:** Blighttown, Depths, Quelaag's Domain · **Bosses:** Chaos Witch Quelaag, Gaping Dragon · **Difficulty:** easy

9. **Wear what you find.** In Blighttown: every armor piece you pick up must be put on at once, and stays on until you leave.
10. **No bonfire.** Do not rest at any bonfire in Blighttown.
11. **Every chest.** In Blighttown: open all 4 chests before you have rung both bells.

## Chapter 4: The Lordvessel

Until you place the Lordvessel · **Areas:** Anor Londo, Firelink Altar, Sen's Fortress, The Great Hollow · **Bosses:** Gwyndolin, Iron Golem, Ornstein and Smough · **Difficulty:** easy

12. **Nothing from the ground.** In Sen's Fortress: nothing you pick up from a corpse or chest may be used or equipped. _Drop anything that is not allowed as soon as you pick it up, so you do not mix it up later._
13. **Dry fight.** Against Iron Golem: do not use Estus.
14. **One bonfire.** In Anor Londo: rest only at the Anor Londo - Residence Bonfire.

## Chapter 5: Lord Soul: Seath

Until you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath) · **Areas:** Ash Lake, Crystal Cave, Painted World of Ariamis, The Duke's Archives · **Bosses:** Crossbreed Priscilla, Seath the Scaleless · **Difficulty:** easy

15. **Nothing from the ground.** In The Duke's Archives: nothing you pick up from a corpse or chest may be used or equipped. Except: Archive Tower Giant Door Key. _Drop anything that is not allowed as soon as you pick it up, so you do not mix it up later._
16. **Counted attempts.** You have 11 attempts against Seath the Scaleless.
17. **Exploit the weakness.** Against Seath the Scaleless: fight only with fire, what it defends worst. Every attack that hits it must deal fire damage. _Weapons, spells and thrown items all count, as long as they deal it._

## Chapter 6: Lord Soul: Bed of Chaos

Until you place the Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos) · **Areas:** Demon Ruins, Lost Izalith, Valley of Drakes · **Bosses:** Bed of Chaos, Ceaseless Discharge, Centipede Demon, Demon Firesage · **Difficulty:** easy

18. **Chapter restriction.** From the moment you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath) until you place the Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos): do not wear rings. Allowed, because this stretch needs them: Orange Charred Ring.
19. **Errand.** Before you place the Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos): take the Gold-Hemmed Black Gloves found in Demon Ruins (Hemmed Black Gloves).

## Chapter 7: Lord Soul: Four Kings

Until you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings) · **Areas:** New Londo Ruins, The Abyss · **Bosses:** Four Kings, Great Grey Wolf Sif · **Difficulty:** medium

20. **Nothing from the ground.** In New Londo Ruins: nothing you pick up from a corpse or chest may be used or equipped. _Drop anything that is not allowed as soon as you pick it up, so you do not mix it up later._
21. **Against the grain.** Against Four Kings: fight only with magic, what it resists most. Every attack that hits it must deal magic damage. _Weapons, spells and thrown items all count, as long as they deal it._

## Chapter 8: Lord Soul: Nito

Until you place the Lord Soul (Nito) · **Areas:** The Catacombs, Tomb of the Giants · **Bosses:** Gravelord Nito, Pinwheel · **Difficulty:** medium

22. **Dry fight.** Against Gravelord Nito: do not use Estus.
23. **Counted attempts.** You have 6 attempts against Gravelord Nito.

## Chapter 9: The First Flame

Until you defeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Areas:** Kiln of the First Flame · **Bosses:** Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Difficulty:** medium

24. **Dry fight.** Against Gwyn, Lord of Cinder: do not use Estus.
25. **Exploit the weakness.** Against Gwyn, Lord of Cinder: fight only with fire, what it defends worst. Every attack that hits it must deal fire damage. _Weapons, spells and thrown items all count, as long as they deal it._

## Definitions

- **Attempt:** each fight against a boss that you start, whether you win, die or leave.
- **Pick up:** take an item from a corpse or a chest in the world. What enemies drop, what you buy and what characters give you are not picked up.
- **Use:** equip, wear, wield, cast or consume.
- **Chest:** a treasure chest placed in the world. A mimic is not one, even though it looks like one.

## Before you go

- **Darkstalker Kaathe:** He appears in the Abyss after the Four Kings only if the Lordvessel is not placed yet. Refuse him or attack him, and he leaves for good.
- **Lautrec of Carim:** Once both bells are rung, or both Fire Keeper Souls (Undead Parish and Blighttown) are taken, Anastacia is killed and Lautrec leaves Firelink, whether or not you freed him. The Firelink Shrine bonfire stays out until her soul is returned to her, a quest that starts with the Black Eye Orb.
- **Trusty Patches:** In the Tomb of the Giants, once Pinwheel is dead or Rhea has set out there, he waits by a ledge and kicks you into the pit below. It is a cutscene, not a death.
- **Rhea of Thorolund:** Once rescued in the Tomb of the Giants, she sells in the Undead Parish. She is lost if two more main bosses die before you visit her, and she leaves once her whole stock is bought.
- **Solaire of Astora:** In Lost Izalith, kill the four Chaos Bugs before you speak to him there, or he is lost to the Sunlight Maggot.
