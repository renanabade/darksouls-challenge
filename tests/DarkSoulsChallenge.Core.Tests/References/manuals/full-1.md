# Dark Souls challenge — seed full-1

- **Class:** Deprived
- **Starting gift:** Master Key
- **Soul level cap:** 35
- **Difficulty:** medium · **Curve:** rollercoaster · **Length:** full

Follow every order. Break one, and the challenge is over: there is no penalty, only the end.

## Rules of the journey

1. **Accumulated curse.** Each boss you defeat adds a new restriction, in this order: (1) Do not wear rings; (2) Do not parry; (3) Do not cast sorceries, miracles or pyromancies; (4) Do not use bows, crossbows or throwing items. Allowed, because the run needs them: Covenant of Artorias, Orange Charred Ring.
2. **Order of the Lords.** Collect the four Lord Souls in this order: Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath), then Lord Soul (Nito), then Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos), then Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings).
3. **Forbidden area.** Do not enter The Great Hollow.
4. **Earned upgrades.** Your weapon may gain only one upgrade level per boss you defeat.

## Chapter 1: The Asylum

Until you defeat Asylum Demon · **Areas:** Northern Undead Asylum · **Bosses:** Asylum Demon · **Difficulty:** hard

5. **Dry fight.** Against Asylum Demon: do not use Estus.

## Chapter 2: The first bell

Until you ring the bell of the Undead Parish · **Areas:** Darkroot Garden, Depths, Firelink Shrine, Undead Burg, Undead Parish · **Bosses:** Bell Gargoyles, Capra Demon, Gaping Dragon, Moonlight Butterfly, Stray Demon, Taurus Demon · **Difficulty:** medium

6. **No bonfire.** Do not rest at any bonfire in Firelink Shrine.

## Chapter 3: The second bell

Until you have rung both bells · **Areas:** Blighttown, Darkroot Basin, Quelaag's Domain, Valley of Drakes · **Bosses:** Chaos Witch Quelaag · **Difficulty:** easy

7. **Assigned weapon.** From the moment you ring the bell of the Undead Parish until you have rung both bells: your weapon is the Zweihander found in Firelink Shrine.
8. **Wear what you find.** In Blighttown: every armor piece you pick up must be put on at once, and stays on until you leave.
9. **Every chest.** In Blighttown: open all 4 chests before you have rung both bells.

## Chapter 4: The Lordvessel

Until you place the Lordvessel · **Areas:** Anor Londo, Firelink Altar, Sen's Fortress · **Bosses:** Gwyndolin, Iron Golem, Ornstein and Smough · **Difficulty:** medium

10. **Nothing from the ground.** In Sen's Fortress: nothing you pick up from a corpse or chest may be used or equipped. _Drop anything that is not allowed as soon as you pick it up, so you do not mix it up later._
11. **The other way.** Until you place the Lordvessel: do not enter Sanctuary Garden.

## Chapter 5: Lord Soul: Seath

Until you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath) · **Areas:** Crystal Cave, The Duke's Archives · **Bosses:** Seath the Scaleless · **Difficulty:** hard

12. **Dry fight.** Against Seath the Scaleless: do not use Estus.

## Chapter 6: Lord Soul: Nito

Until you place the Lord Soul (Nito) · **Areas:** The Catacombs, Tomb of the Giants · **Bosses:** Gravelord Nito, Pinwheel · **Difficulty:** easy

13. **Errand.** Before you place the Lord Soul (Nito): take the Covetous Silver Serpent Ring found in Tomb of the Giants.

## Chapter 7: Lord Soul: Bed of Chaos

Until you place the Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos) · **Areas:** Demon Ruins, Lost Izalith, Oolacile Sanctuary, Painted World of Ariamis, Royal Wood · **Bosses:** Bed of Chaos, Ceaseless Discharge, Centipede Demon, Crossbreed Priscilla, Demon Firesage, Artorias · **Difficulty:** medium

14. **Wear what you find.** In Demon Ruins: every armor piece you pick up must be put on at once, and stays on until you leave.

## Chapter 8: Lord Soul: Four Kings

Until you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings) · **Areas:** Chasm of the Abyss, New Londo Ruins, Oolacile Township, The Abyss · **Bosses:** Black Dragon Kalameet, Four Kings, Great Grey Wolf Sif, Manus, Father of the Abyss · **Difficulty:** hard

15. **Counted attempts.** You have 5 attempts against Great Grey Wolf Sif.
16. **Chapter restriction.** From the moment you place the Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos) until you place the Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings): do not reinforce your weapons.

## Chapter 9: The First Flame

Until you defeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Areas:** Kiln of the First Flame · **Bosses:** Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Difficulty:** medium

17. **Counted attempts.** You have 8 attempts against Gwyn, Lord of Cinder.
18. **Errand.** Before you defeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder: take the Black Knight Armor found in Kiln of the First Flame.

## Definitions

- **Attempt:** each fight against a boss that you start, whether you win, die or leave.
- **Pick up:** take an item from a corpse or a chest in the world. What enemies drop, what you buy and what characters give you are not picked up.
- **Use:** equip, wear, wield, cast or consume.
- **Chest:** a treasure chest placed in the world. A mimic is not one, even though it looks like one.

## Before you go

- **Darkstalker Kaathe:** He appears in the Abyss after the Four Kings only if the Lordvessel is not placed yet. Refuse him or attack him, and he leaves for good.
- **Lautrec of Carim:** Once both bells are rung, or both Fire Keeper Souls (Undead Parish and Blighttown) are taken, Anastacia is killed and Lautrec leaves Firelink, whether or not you freed him. The Firelink Shrine bonfire stays out until her soul is returned to her, a quest that starts with the Black Eye Orb.
- **Trusty Patches:** In the Tomb of the Giants, once Pinwheel is dead or Rhea has set out there, he waits by a ledge and kicks you into the pit below. It is a cutscene, not a death.
- **Rhea of Thorolund:** Once rescued in the Tomb of the Giants, she sells in the Undead Parish. She is lost if two more main bosses die before you visit her, and she leaves once her whole stock is bought.
- **Solaire of Astora:** In Lost Izalith, kill the four Chaos Bugs before you speak to him there, or he is lost to the Sunlight Maggot.
