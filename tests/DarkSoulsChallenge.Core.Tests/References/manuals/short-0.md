# Dark Souls challenge — seed short-0

- **Class:** Warrior
- **Starting gift:** Black Firebomb x10
- **Soul level cap:** 60
- **Difficulty:** easy · **Curve:** rollercoaster · **Length:** short

Follow every order. Break one, and the challenge is over: there is no penalty, only the end.

## Rules of the journey

1. **Closed merchant.** Do not buy anything from Quelana of Izalith.
2. **Phase swap.** After the Asylum Demon and until you have rung both bells: only thrusting swords. After it: drop what does not fit and follow only daggers.
3. **Order of the Lords.** Collect the four Lord Souls in this order: Lord Soul (Bed of Chaos), then Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Four Kings), then Bequeathed Lord Soul Shard (Seath), then Lord Soul (Nito).
4. **Forbidden area.** Do not enter Royal Wood.

## Act 1: The first bell

Until you ring the bell of the Undead Parish · **Areas:** Darkroot Garden, Firelink Shrine, Northern Undead Asylum, Undead Burg, Undead Parish · **Bosses:** Asylum Demon, Bell Gargoyles, Capra Demon, Moonlight Butterfly, Stray Demon, Taurus Demon · **Difficulty:** easy

5. **Wear what you find.** In Undead Burg: every armor piece you pick up must be put on at once, and stays on until you leave.

## Act 2: The Lordvessel

Until you place the Lordvessel · **Areas:** Anor Londo, Blighttown, Darkroot Basin, Depths, Firelink Altar, Quelaag's Domain, Sen's Fortress, The Great Hollow · **Bosses:** Chaos Witch Quelaag, Gaping Dragon, Gwyndolin, Iron Golem, Ornstein and Smough · **Difficulty:** medium

6. **Dry fight.** Against Gaping Dragon: do not use Estus.
7. **One bonfire.** In Anor Londo: rest only at the Darkmoon Tomb Bonfire.

## Act 3: The Lord Souls

Until you place the Lord Soul (Nito) · **Areas:** Ash Lake, Crystal Cave, Demon Ruins, Lost Izalith, New Londo Ruins, Oolacile Sanctuary, Painted World of Ariamis, Sanctuary Garden, The Abyss, The Catacombs, The Duke's Archives, Tomb of the Giants, Valley of Drakes · **Bosses:** Bed of Chaos, Ceaseless Discharge, Centipede Demon, Crossbreed Priscilla, Demon Firesage, Four Kings, Gravelord Nito, Great Grey Wolf Sif, Pinwheel, Sanctuary Guardian, Seath the Scaleless · **Difficulty:** easy

8. **Nothing from the ground.** In The Duke's Archives: nothing you pick up from a corpse or chest may be used or equipped. Except: Archive Tower Giant Door Key. _Drop anything that is not allowed as soon as you pick it up, so you do not mix it up later._
9. **Counted attempts.** You have 10 attempts against Gravelord Nito.

## Act 4: The First Flame

Until you defeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Areas:** Kiln of the First Flame · **Bosses:** Gwyn, Lord of Cinder · **Difficulty:** medium

10. **Counted attempts.** You have 7 attempts against Gwyn, Lord of Cinder.

## Definitions

- **Attempt:** each fight against a boss that you start, whether you win, die or leave.
- **Pick up:** take an item from a corpse or a chest in the world. What enemies drop, what you buy and what characters give you are not picked up.
- **Use:** equip, wear, wield, cast or consume.

## Before you go

- **Darkstalker Kaathe:** He appears in the Abyss after the Four Kings only if the Lordvessel is not placed yet. Refuse him or attack him, and he leaves for good.
- **Lautrec of Carim:** Once both bells are rung, or both Fire Keeper Souls (Undead Parish and Blighttown) are taken, Anastacia is killed and Lautrec leaves Firelink, whether or not you freed him. The Firelink Shrine bonfire stays out until her soul is returned to her, a quest that starts with the Black Eye Orb.
- **Trusty Patches:** In the Tomb of the Giants, once Pinwheel is dead or Rhea has set out there, he waits by a ledge and kicks you into the pit below. It is a cutscene, not a death.
- **Rhea of Thorolund:** Once rescued in the Tomb of the Giants, she sells in the Undead Parish. She is lost if two more main bosses die before you visit her, and she leaves once her whole stock is bought.
- **Solaire of Astora:** In Lost Izalith, kill the four Chaos Bugs before you speak to him there, or he is lost to the Sunlight Maggot.
